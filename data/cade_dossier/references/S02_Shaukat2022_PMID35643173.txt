Computer-Aided Detection Improves Adenomas per Colonoscopy for Screening and Surveillance Colonoscopy: A Randomized Trial

Shaukat 2022 (SKOUT) | Gastroenterology 163(3):732-741 (2022)
PMID: 35643173 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2022.05.028
PMC full text: (not open access — paywalled)

Abstract
Multicentre RCT (SKOUT): CADe increased adenomas per colonoscopy (1.05 vs 0.83, p=.002) without raising non-neoplastic resections; ADR 47.8% vs 43.9% (p=.065).
