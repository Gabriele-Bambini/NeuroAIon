# CADe colonoscopy & ADR — review package

Generated by **NeuroAIon** from your master dossier + PubMed/PMC metadata.

## What's here
- `review/paper.pdf` — the journal-formatted systematic review & meta-analysis (open this).
- `review/paper.tex` + `references.bib` — LaTeX source (compile for the typeset version).
- `review/figures/` — forest, funnel, PRISMA flow, risk-of-bias (vector PDF + PNG).
- `review/documents/` — full PRISMA dossier: protocol, search log, screening log,
  RoB, GRADE Summary of Findings, PRISMA 27-item + abstract checklists, declarations.
- `review/report.md`, `review/review.html`, `review/state.json` — machine-readable outputs.
- `references/` — the 8 included trials: `cade_references.ris` (import-ready) + per-study
  metadata/abstract files with PMID, DOI and PMC link.
- `fetch_oa_fulltext.py` — run on your own machine to download the 6 open-access full
  texts into `review_pdfs/`; the 2 paywalled trials need your institutional access.

## Headline result (real data)
Pooled risk ratio for ADR with CADe vs standard colonoscopy: **RR 1.243 (95% CI 1.130–1.367)**,
random-effects (REML + Hartung–Knapp), I² = 42%, k = 8, 6503 participants. GRADE certainty: **Low**.

## To enrich with full texts
1. `python fetch_oa_fulltext.py`  →  populates `review_pdfs/`
2. set `fulltext_dir: "review_pdfs"` in your protocol and re-run the pipeline.
