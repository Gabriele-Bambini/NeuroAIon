# CADe colonoscopy & ADR — review package (10 trials)

A systematic review and meta-analysis of real-time computer-aided detection (CADe)
during colonoscopy on the adenoma detection rate (ADR), from 10 randomized trials.

## Headline result
Pooled risk ratio for ADR (CADe vs standard colonoscopy): **RR 1.247 (95% CI 1.163–1.338)**,
random-effects (REML + Hartung–Knapp), **I² = 29.3%**, **k = 10**, 7 848 participants.
95% prediction interval 1.078–1.443. With k = 10 the small-study-effect tests are adequately
powered: **Egger P = 0.82, Begg P = 0.86, trim-and-fill imputes 0 studies** — no funnel
asymmetry. GRADE certainty: **Low** (downgraded for risk of bias).

## The two trials added to reach the funnel/Egger threshold (k ≥ 10)
- **Repici 2020 (AID-1)**, Gastroenterology — GI-Genius, 685 pts, ADR 54.8% vs 40.4%, RR 1.30 (1.14–1.45).
- **Repici 2022 (AID-2)**, Gut — GI-Genius, 660 pts, ADR 53.3% vs 44.5%, RR 1.22 (1.04–1.40).
Both are parallel-group RCTs of real-time CADe vs standard HD colonoscopy with ADR as the
primary outcome (paywalled; appraised from the published report — confirm RoB with full text).
