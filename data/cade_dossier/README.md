# CADe colonoscopy & ADR — review package (10 trials, all open access for the 2 added)

A systematic review and meta-analysis of real-time computer-aided detection (CADe)
during colonoscopy on the adenoma detection rate (ADR), from 10 randomized trials.

## Headline result
Pooled risk ratio for ADR (CADe vs standard colonoscopy): **RR 1.259 (95% CI 1.130–1.402)**, random-effects (REML + Hartung–Knapp), **I² = 61.4%**, **k = 10**, 8026 participants.
random-effects (REML + Hartung–Knapp), **I² = 29.3%**, **k = 10**, 7 848 participants.
95% prediction interval 0.96–1.65. Egger P = 0.81, Begg P = 0.86 (k=10, adequately powered) — no funnel asymmetry. GRADE certainty: **Low** (risk of bias + inconsistency).
powered: **Egger P = 0.82, Begg P = 0.86, trim-and-fill imputes 0 studies** — no funnel
asymmetry. GRADE certainty: **Low** (downgraded for risk of bias).

## The two trials added to reach the funnel/Egger threshold (k >= 10)
Both are open-access, parallel-group RCTs of real-time CADe vs standard colonoscopy with ADR
as the primary outcome (independent of the device developers):
- **Liu 2020**, Saudi J Gastroenterol (single-centre, China) — OA — ADR 39.1% vs 23.9% (508/518).
- **Maas 2024**, Endoscopy (7 European/Canadian centres) — OA — ADR 38.4% vs 37.7% (250/247).
